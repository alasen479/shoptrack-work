How to Get More Customers Through WhatsApp in Cameroon

WhatsApp is the most used business communication tool in Cameroon. The businesses that grow use it proactively.

WhatsApp is not just for chatting. In Cameroon, it is the primary channel through which most small businesses communicate with customers, share products, confirm orders, and collect payments. The difference between businesses that grow and those that stay flat is often just this: one uses WhatsApp proactively, the other reacts to it.

The reactive vs proactive difference

A reactive business responds when customers message them. A proactive business sends customers their catalogue before they ask, confirms every appointment, sends payment reminders, and wishes clients happy birthday. The proactive business stays top of mind.

Payment reminders without the awkwardness

Chasing customers for money owed is uncomfortable when it is personal. ShopTrack sends a professional payment reminder from the business — not from you personally. The tone is right and the awkwardness is removed.

According to GSMA Africa digital communications data, WhatsApp has the highest penetration rate of any messaging platform across Sub-Saharan Africa, with Cameroon among the top countries by usage per capita. World Bank SME research confirms that digital customer communication tools are among the highest-ROI investments available to small businesses.
